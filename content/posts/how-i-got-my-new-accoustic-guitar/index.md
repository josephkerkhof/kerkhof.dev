One of the most important things in my life is music performance. I started playing the violin when I was four years old
with the [Suzuki method](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suzuki_method) and completed the method and graduated book ten
when I was a senior in high school. I consider it one of my life's biggest achievements.

When I was a freshman in high school, I started playing guitar. I guess I wanted to play something a bit more popular
than Vivaldi and Mozart.

Since then, I've been playing on the same cheap Alvarez acoustic guitar that I picked up at an estate sale for $150.

Recently, I started playing acoustic guitar for my local church, and now that I have a little purchasing power, decided
it is time to finally get a decent guitar.

I went down to the Guitar Center and compared several off the rack. I ended up liking the Martin D-28 the best, so I
bought it! Was very excited to take it to a guitar luthier to have electronics installed in it.

I took it to a local shop, and they immediately told me that the neck set on my brand-new guitar was bad. It came from
the manufacturer already bad! This would be a $700 repair! Devastation! The guitar I was so excited about now had this
fatal flaw.

After getting three different opinions on it, they all said the same thing. But the third luthier I spoke with had a
well-taken-care-of Martin D-35 50th anniversary edition for sale! He showed me and I immediately fell in love with it.

![Front of my Martin D-35 50th Anniversary Edition](martin-d35-front.webp)
![Three-piece back of my Martin D-35 50th Anniversary Edition](martin-d35-back.webp)

_isn't it pretty?_

What immediately jumped out at me was the slight flame on the soundboard of the guitar and the wave in the three-piece
back. When I played it, it had a warmer, fuller sound than the D-28.

In the end, it was an easy decision to return the D-28 to Guitar Center for a full refund.

The used 50th anniversary edition D-35 was cheaper than the new D-28 from Guitar Center! And it’s actually _better_
suited to the music I perform (hymns at church).

I get some serious joy out of playing this instrument, and I feel very blessed to own it.

Next time, I’m starting at a local shop. Having someone who deeply knows these instruments and their inventory helped me
find the right one.
